# 1. Getting set up

This section is for the first time you use Roman SMS. If you have
used it before and just need to log in, jump to "Logging in again"
at the end.

## How you get an account

Roman SMS is not a sign-up-and-go service. The operator creates your
organization and your login personally. This is deliberate: it means
someone has checked that your organization is real, that your brand
prefix is available, and that your first login will work.

The sequence is:

1. **You apply.** On the Roman SMS website, click **Sign up** and
   fill in the form — your organization's name, a short name for the
   web address (the "slug"), your name, your email, your phone, and
   your address. Submit.
2. **You receive a confirmation email.** This just tells you the
   application was received. It does not give you a login yet.
3. **The operator reviews and approves.** Usually within a working
   day. If there is a problem with the application, the operator
   contacts you by email or phone before approving.
4. **You receive a welcome email with a link.** The link lets you set
   your password for the first time. **It expires in 24 hours.** If
   it expires, contact the operator to send a new one.
5. **You set your password.** See the next section.

## Setting your password for the first time

The welcome email contains a link that looks something like
`https://romansms.com/auth/password-reset/…`. Click it (or paste
it into your browser).

You land on a page asking for a new password. Type it twice.

**What makes a good password:**

- At least 8 characters. Longer is better.
- Something you don't use on any other website. If someone guesses
  your Roman SMS password, they could send messages on your school's
  behalf and spend your credits.
- Not a name, a birthday, or a simple word.

Click **Set password**. You are taken to the login page. Log in with
the email address the operator has on file and your new password.

**If the link has expired** (more than 24 hours have passed), it will
say so when you click. Contact the operator — they can send a fresh
link from their side. Do not try to guess a workaround; the link is
designed to expire for security, and a new one takes less than a
minute to send.

## What to check the first time you log in

Once you are logged in, take a moment to check a few things.

**Your organization's name and brand prefix.** Look at the top of the
page. You should see your school's or business's name. The brand
prefix — the short identifier added to every message you send — is
shown on the message screen. Confirm with the operator that this is
right before you send your first campaign. The brand prefix is what
your recipients see in front of your message; if it's wrong, they
might not recognize the sender.

**Your wallet balance.** On the dashboard (see Section 2), find your
wallet balance. This is how many credits you have. If it says 0, you
need to buy credits (Section 8) before you can send.

**Your staff list.** If you're the administrator, you can add your
staff members (Section 7). If you're a staff member, ask your
administrator to add you if you haven't been added yet.

## Logging in again

Every visit after the first:

1. Go to `https://romansms.com`.
2. Click **Log in** in the top-right.
3. Enter your email and password.
4. Click **Log in**.

You will be taken to your dashboard.

**If you forgot your password:** click **Forgot password?** on the
login page. Enter your email. You'll receive a link to set a new
password. If you don't receive it within a few minutes, check your
spam folder, then contact the operator.

**If your account is not active:** you'll see a message saying your
account has been suspended or terminated. Contact your school's
administrator if you are a staff member, or the operator if you are
the administrator. Someone has deliberately changed your access, and
they're the only one who can change it back.

## What you can and cannot do

Your account has a **role**, which determines what you can see and
do. There are three roles:

- **Master** — the operator. You don't have this role unless you run
  Roman SMS itself.
- **School or business administrator** — full control of your
  organization. Can manage users, buy credits, see all data, and send
  to anyone.
- **Staff member** — can send messages, manage contacts, and use the
  system, but cannot manage other users or see billing. If your staff
  member has a **department**, they only see contacts and campaigns
  belonging to their department or shared across the school.

You can see your own role on your **Profile** page. If you think your
role is wrong, contact the operator — roles are set when the account
is created and don't change often.
