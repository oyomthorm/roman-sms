# Welcome to Roman SMS

Roman SMS sends text messages to your school's or business's
contacts — parents, guardians, staff, customers, members. You write
a message, choose who receives it, and we deliver it. This manual is
for the person who runs Roman SMS for your organization: the school
administrator or the business owner. If you are a staff member who
only sends messages, you can read Sections 3 through 6 and skip the
rest.

## What you can do with Roman SMS

- **Send messages to everyone, one group, or one district.** One
  message, many recipients.
- **Send a different message to each person**, using names or balances
  you have in a spreadsheet.
- **Schedule messages to go out later**, so you don't have to be at a
  computer when they need to send.
- **Keep contacts organized** in groups — "Form 1 Parents", "Class 6",
  "Staff", "Regular customers".
- **Import contacts from a spreadsheet** in one step, and see exactly
  which rows went in and which were skipped.
- **Add your own staff**, each with their own login and their own
  department.
- **Track every message and every credit** you spend.
- **Chat directly with us** if you need help.

## What Roman SMS is not

- **It does not receive messages.** Your recipients cannot reply back
  through Roman SMS. If they want to reach you, they use your school
  phone number or your email.
- **It does not do email, voice calls, or social media.** Only text
  messages.
- **It does not sell you credits automatically.** You contact the
  operator to buy more. (Section 8 explains how this works.)

## A word about credits

Every message you send costs **credits**. One credit sends one
**segment** — a segment is roughly 160 characters (or 70 characters
if your message has non-English characters like emoji). A long
message becomes two segments and costs two credits; a very long one
becomes three, and so on.

Your organization's **brand prefix** — a short identifier like
"FITTING:" — is added to the front of every message and counts toward
the length. The message screen shows you the exact cost before you
send, so you never get a surprise.

Your balance lives in your **wallet**. When you buy a plan, credits
go into your wallet. Every message you send takes credits out. You
can see the balance and the history any time.

## How to use this manual

Each section covers one part of the system. You can read them in
order, or jump to the one you need. If you are new, start with
Section 1 (Getting set up). If you're trying to do something specific,
the table below shows where to look.

| I want to… | Read |
|---|---|
| Log in for the first time | Section 1 |
| Understand what I'm seeing on the home page | Section 2 |
| Add contacts, one at a time or from a file | Section 3 |
| Organize my contacts | Section 4 |
| Send a message now | Section 5 |
| Send a message later (or on a schedule) | Section 6 |
| Add my staff members | Section 7 |
| Check my balance, buy credits, see invoices | Section 8 |
| Get help from the operator | Section 9 |
| Fix a problem | Section 10 |

Throughout this manual:

- **Bold text** is a button, link, or field label on the screen.
- `Monospaced text` is something you type exactly as shown.
- **Admin only** marks a section that only the school or business
  administrator can use. If you are a staff member, you can skip it.
- `[SCREENSHOT]` marks a place where a picture of the screen could
  be added in a future printing. The text describes the screen well
  enough without it.

## Who to contact

If you need help — you can't log in, you think a message didn't send,
you need to buy credits — contact the operator. The fastest way is
the **Chat** page inside Roman SMS. It goes straight to us.
