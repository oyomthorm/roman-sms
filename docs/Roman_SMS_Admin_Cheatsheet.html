Your wallet

Credits = money for SMS. 1 credit = 1 SMS segment (~160 chars).

Days left on the dashboard estimates runway at current spend.

Low balance → contact the operator via Chat. No "Buy" button exists.

Plan expired? Your credits are safe — they don't expire. But you can't send until renewed.

Three ways to send

  • Standard — one body, one group. Most sends.
  • Personalized — CSV with per-row values in {{name}}, {{balance}}. Cost = sum of per-row segments.
  • Paste numbers — up to 500 pasted numbers, one-off. Skips opt-outs.

Counter shows cost before you send. Includes brand prefix.
