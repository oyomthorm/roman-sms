1. Prepare your file

Your CSV must have a header row with a phone number column. Recognised headers: phone, number, mobile, msisdn. Optional columns: name, group, district.

Phone numbers can be in any of these forms — they are all treated as the same number: 0700123456, 700123456, 256700123456, +256700123456, 00256700123456.

Landlines (starting 041, 043, etc.) are rejected because SMS cannot be delivered to them.
